How it works

From “I need a hand” to “done and rated”

One account does both. Post work when you need help, apply when you have time.

Create your account

Post the work

Describe the task, drop a pin on the map, set pay per person and how many people you need. Your exact address stays private until you accept someone.

For makers

Get matched with people nearby

People within your radius see the job with a distance. They apply with a short note, and you compare their level, rating and past work.

For makersFor workers

Do the work and stay in touch

Accept the people you like, chat in the app, then start the job. Online pay is released from the maker's wallet only when the job is marked complete.

For makersFor workers

Get paid, get rated

Earnings land in the worker's wallet minus a small platform fee. Both sides rate each other, and reviews go public together so nobody is rated out of revenge.
